---

## 📌 Overview

A visual storytelling project built on Airbnb listings in Milan. The story follows a young Brazilian football player who has just signed his first professional contract with a club in Milan, and his family (parents, sister and brother) who are visiting him for the first time and want to stay close by.

Each family member has different interests, from football and home cooking to concerts, shopping and nightlife, so the question becomes: **where in Milan can everyone be happy, and which listing fits them best?**

---

## ❓ Guiding Questions

- Where do the family's points of interest (San Siro, City Center and Duomo, CityLife Mall, Forum Assago, Linate Airport, nightlife) point to?
- Is Navigli "the place to be", and is it worth its price compared with the rest of Municipio 6?
- Which three listings best match the family's needs and budget?

---

## 🔍 Approach

1. **Personas:** four family members with distinct interests, built to define what the stay needs to deliver.
2. **Market context:** the evolution of Airbnb in Milan.
3. **Location:** mapping the points of interest, which all point to **Municipio 6**, then comparing listing counts across its neighborhoods.
4. **Price check:** Navigli is slightly overpriced within Municipio 6, but offers a similar price range with about 4x the listings. Nightly prices are capped at €600, and the budget line is set at €200.
5. **Filtering:** within walking distance of Porta Genova, applying these criteria:
   - Entire property (entire home/apartment)
   - 4 adults, 3+ beds
   - Kitchen
   - Under €200 per night
   - 2+ bathrooms and 2+ bedrooms
   - Superhost
6. **Final shortlist:** three listings compared on price per night and review scores (communication, value, check-in, cleanliness, accuracy, location).

---

## 🏁 The Final 3 Listings

| Listing | Price per night |
|---|---|
| "A terrace above Naviglio canal" | €117.14 |
| "Charming apartment in Navigli District" | €179.55 |
| "Navigli-Bocconi downtown kid-friendly" | €148.77 |
